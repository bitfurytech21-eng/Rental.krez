## 1. No Guarantee of Principal or Rental Returns

- **Target Yields Are Estimates Only:** Any projected net rental yield, annual cashflow schedule, appreciation forecast, or Internal Rate of Return (IRR) displayed on the Platform represents an illustrative underwriting model based on assumptions that may fail to materialize.
- **Past Performance Is Not Indicative of Future Results:** Historical rental collection, realized exits, or prior property performance do not guarantee future income or capital preservation.
- **Variable Distributions:** Monthly or periodic rental distributions depend strictly on actual net rent collected after property taxes, insurance, property management, maintenance reserves, and SPV administrative costs. Distributions may be reduced, delayed, or suspended entirely during periods of tenant vacancy or extraordinary building repairs.

## 2. Real Estate Market, Property & Tenant Risks

Underlying real estate assets located in France, Spain, or other European jurisdictions are exposed to macroeconomic and property-specific risks:

1. **Market & Valuation Fluctuations:** Property market prices may decline due to interest rate changes, credit tightening, inflation, macroeconomic downturns, or shifts in local real estate demand.
2. **Tenant Default & Vacancy Risk:** Tenants may fail to pay rent on time, enter insolvency, or vacate at lease expiry, reducing or interrupting distributable rental income and increasing re-letting expenses.
3. **CapEx & Unforeseen Maintenance:** Structural defects, environmental remediation, energy-efficiency compliance upgrades, or uninsured casualties may require unbudgeted capital expenditure that reduces net distributions or exit proceeds.
4. **Regulatory, Zoning & Rent-Control Changes:** Changes in municipal short-term/long-term rental licensing, rent-cap legislation, property taxation, or urban zoning rules can adversely impact property cashflows and resale value.

## 3. Illiquidity & Long-Term Investment Horizon

Real estate is an **illiquid, medium-to-long-term asset class**:

- **No Guaranteed Liquidity or Exit Window:** You should be prepared to hold your SPV participation for the full intended investment horizon until the underlying property is refinanced or sold.
- **No Assured Secondary Market:** Although the Platform records fractional positions and may support compliant transfers among KYC-verified investors, there is no guarantee that an active secondary market, matching buyer, or liquidity desk will exist when you wish to exit.
- **No On-Demand Redemption:** Invested SPV capital cannot generally be redeemed on demand prior to asset disposal.

## 4. SPV Structuring, Tax & Regulatory Risks

- **Corporate & SPV Risk:** Each offering is structured via a ring-fenced SPV intended to isolate property assets from unrelated projects and the Platform operator (SIREN 494 861 560 · Paris R.C.S.). Nevertheless, administrative, legal, or insolvency proceedings involving an SPV, property manager, or banking partner could delay distributions or asset liquidation.
- **Evolving Regulatory Landscape:** European Union, French, and Spanish rules governing fractionalized real estate, crowdfunding (ECSP), financial instruments, and crypto-assets (MiCA) continue to evolve. Regulatory changes may require modification of transfer mechanics, reporting requirements, or offering availability in specific jurisdictions.
- **Taxation Risk:** Individual tax treatment depends on your personal circumstances and country of residence and may change without notice. Cross-border investments may trigger withholding taxes or double-taxation reporting obligations.

## 5. Digital Assets, Stablecoins & Self-Custody Risks

Where deposits, distributions, or compliance workflows involve blockchain rails (including Solana USDC, SPL Token, or Token-2022 standards) or Platform-assigned digital payment channels:

- **Stablecoin & Peg Risk:** Stablecoins such as USDC are issued by third-party entities and rely on reserve management and banking partners. Temporary de-pegging, issuer freezes, or regulatory restrictions could affect settlement value or liquidity.
- **Network & Protocol Risks:** Public blockchains may experience congestion, fee spikes, validator downtime, or smart-contract vulnerabilities outside our control.
- **Irreversible Transactions & Wallet Security:** Blockchain transfers are generally irreversible. Sending funds on the wrong network, to an incorrect address, or losing access to your self-custodial wallet private keys (or having your wallet compromised) can result in permanent, unrecoverable loss of assets.
- **Distinction Between USDC and Property Rights:** Holding USDC or other digital currencies in a personal wallet does **not** confer ownership in any property SPV; property economic rights arise solely from verified subscriptions recorded under an executed Investment Agreement.

## 6. Payment, Settlement & Counterparty Risks

Bank transfers (SEPA / SWIFT) and manual payment verification rails rely on third-party financial institutions and payment processors. Errors in transfer references, incomplete payment proof, AML/compliance holds, intermediary bank fees, or banking holidays can delay credit to your Platform ledger or external withdrawals.
