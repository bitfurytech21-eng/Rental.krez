## 2. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the personal data strictly necessary to operate the Platform, verify investor identity, process deposits and distributions, and satisfy legal obligations:

- **Account & Identification Data:** Full legal name, email address, telephone number, nationality, date of birth, tax residency country, and account preferences.
- **KYC / AML Compliance Data:** Government-issued passport or national identity document, proof of residential address, corporate registry extracts (such as Kbis or Nota Simple), Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) declarations, and source-of-funds / source-of-wealth documentation where required.

- **Communications & Support Records:** Inquiries submitted via our contact forms, investor support desk, or Smartsupp live chat (when enabled with your consent).
- **Technical & Security Data:** Authentication session tokens, device/browser metadata, IP-based rate-limiting and brute-force protection logs, and audit trails for sensitive account or legal-acceptance actions.

## 3. Purposes & Legal Bases for Processing

Under Article 6 of the GDPR, we process your personal data strictly on the following legal bases:

1. **Performance of a Contract (Article 6(1)(b)):** Creating and administering your investor account, granting data-room access, executing SPV Investment Agreements, crediting verified deposits, calculating rental distributions, and processing withdrawals.
2. **Compliance with Legal Obligations (Article 6(1)(c)):** Conducting mandatory Customer Due Diligence (CDD), Politically Exposed Person (PEP) and sanctions screening under EU AMLD5/AMLD6, maintaining statutory financial and tax accounting records, and responding to lawful requests from financial or supervisory authorities.
3. **Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)):** Protecting the Platform against fraud, unauthorized access, bot abuse, and payment tampering; maintaining system security and audit logs; and improving platform reliability.
4. **Your Prior Consent (Article 6(1)(a)):** Deploying non-essential cookies, performance analytics, embedded third-party media, and optional live-chat widgets (such as Smartsupp), as well as sending optional investor updates where you opt in.

## 8. Your Rights Under the GDPR

Subject to applicable statutory exceptions (including mandatory AML retention obligations), you have the right to:

- **Access:** Obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and receive a copy of it.
- **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
- **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of personal data where there is no overriding legal obligation requiring us to retain it.
- **Restriction of Processing:** Request temporary suspension of processing in specific circumstances.
- **Data Portability:** Receive structured, machine-readable copies of personal data you provided to us on the basis of contract or consent.
- **Objection & Consent Withdrawal:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests or withdraw consent at any time for optional cookies or communications.

To exercise any of these rights, email **legal@kretz.site** or **info@kretz.site** from your registered email address. We respond within one (1) month in accordance with GDPR Article 12.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent EU data protection supervisory authority, including the **CNIL** (*Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés*, France — `https://www.cnil.fr`) or the **AEPD** (*Agencia Española de Protección de Datos*, Spain — `https://www.aepd.es`).
